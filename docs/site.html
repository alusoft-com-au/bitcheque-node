A savings chain一條儲蓄鏈

Savings you sign with a pen.用一支筆就能簽名的儲蓄。

Your key is a printed book. No device ever holds it, no device ever signs with it. You copy 36 cells, hand them to any computer, and the chain checks hashes. Nothing else.你的金鑰是一本印好的簿子。沒有任何裝置持有它，也沒有任何裝置用它簽名。你抄 36 格，交給任何一台電腦，鏈只驗證雜湊值，別無其他。

Why it exists為什麼要有它

The device was the weak point.裝置才是弱點。

In October 2026 a batch of hardware wallets shipped with an implant that read seeds off the screen. Every design that trusts one device fails the same way. Bitcheque removes the device from signing entirely.2026 年 10 月，一批硬體錢包出廠時被植入了讀取螢幕種子的元件。任何信任單一裝置的設計都會以同樣方式失守。Bitcheque 把裝置從簽名過程中徹底拿掉。

Paper is the key紙就是金鑰

A book is printed in two unrelated places, each half useless alone. Signing is copying cells with a pen. The computer that broadcasts can be anyone's.簿子在兩個不相關的地方印出，各半本單獨無用。簽名就是用筆抄格子。負責廣播的電腦可以是任何人的。

Hashes only只有雜湊

The chain stores one hash per cell and compares. No signatures to verify, no curves to break, nothing a quantum computer gets a shortcut into.鏈上每格存一個雜湊值，只做比對。沒有簽章要驗、沒有曲線可破，量子電腦也找不到捷徑。

Nothing to remember不必記任何東西

The chain tracks which cheques are spent. Lose the books, reprint from the seeds. Lose a device, nothing happens. There is no counter, file or app state to back up.鏈記錄哪些支票已用。簿子丟了就從種子重印；裝置丟了什麼事都沒有。沒有計數器、檔案或 App 狀態需要備份。

How it works運作方式

Print once. Move ten times.印一次，轉十次。

Print two halves印出兩半

Your phone and home printer make half-book A from seed A. A library PC and its printer make half-book B. Neither sees the other's half.手機加家中印表機用種子 A 印出半本 A；圖書館電腦和它的印表機印出半本 B。彼此看不到對方那一半。

Register登記

Any computer combines the two public hash files and registers the book, paid by a minute of proof-of-work or a fee cheque from a friend. You get a number.任何電腦合併兩份公開雜湊檔並登記簿子，用一分鐘的工作量證明或朋友的手續費支票支付。你得到一個帳號。

Receive by number用帳號收款

Give out 12 digits, the last one a check digit the chain enforces. A mis-copied digit fails instead of paying a stranger.給對方 12 位數字，最後一位是鏈強制檢查的檢查碼。抄錯一位會作廢，而不是付給陌生人。

Sign with a pen用筆簽名

Write payee and amount, copy the 36 cells those digits point at, hand them to any computer. It can only send exactly that payment.寫下收款人和金額，抄下那些數字指到的 36 格，交給任何電腦。它只能照原樣送出這筆付款。

Who it is for給誰用

A safe, not a wallet.是保險箱，不是錢包。

Savers儲蓄者

Ten moves per book, years apart. Keep large holdings across several books in several places. Spend daily money somewhere else.每本十次轉移，相隔數年。大額資產分散在多本簿子、多個地點。日常花費放在別處。

Miners礦工

SHA-256d proof-of-work, merge-mineable with Bitcoin at no extra cost. Per-block difficulty. No premine, slow-start reward, same 21M schedule.SHA-256d 工作量證明，可與比特幣合併挖礦、不增成本。逐區塊調整難度。無預挖，獎勵緩步啟動，同樣的 2,100 萬發行曲線。

Builders開發者

Five transaction types, one hash function, a plain-text RPC. The whole node fits in an afternoon of reading.五種交易、一個雜湊函數、純文字 RPC。整個節點一個下午就能讀完。

Build開發

Run a node in one command.一行指令跑起節點。

The node is Rust with no dependencies, including its own 53-line SHA-256 checked against the standard vectors. Consensus is small enough to audit line by line.節點以 Rust 寫成、零依賴，連 SHA-256 都是自帶的 53 行實作並通過標準測試向量。共識程式碼小到可以逐行審計。

cargo build --release
./target/release/bitcheque genesis
./target/release/bitcheque node --testnet \
  --data chain.dat --listen 0.0.0.0:8731 \
  --rpc 127.0.0.1:8732 --mine 1

A wallet talks to the node over plain HTTP. Every answer is text you can read in a browser.錢包透過純 HTTP 和節點溝通。每個回應都是瀏覽器裡能直接讀的文字。

Questions常見問題

Straight answers.直接的回答。

Is it quantum-safe?抗量子嗎？

Yes, at the level NIST accepts: forging a cell needs a preimage of SHA-256, 2^128 work classically and 2^64 sequential steps on a quantum computer. There are no elliptic curves anywhere in consensus.是，達到 NIST 接受的等級：偽造一格需要找 SHA-256 的原像，傳統計算 2^128 次，量子電腦 2^64 個循序步驟。共識中沒有任何橢圓曲線。

What if I lose a book?簿子丟了怎麼辦？

Reprint it from its seed. Before using the reprint, confirm one zero-coin cheque numbered above anything the old book held, then destroy the old book if it turns up. Losing a seed and its book together loses that half, so keep them apart.用它的種子重印。使用重印本前，先確認一張號碼高於舊簿所有號碼的 0 幣支票，舊簿若找到就銷毀。種子和簿子一起遺失才會失去那一半，所以要分開放。

Is this safer than Bitcoin?比比特幣安全嗎？

Different, and honest about it. Bitcoin's weak point is the signing device and, later, quantum computers. Bitcheque's weak point is paper: whoever holds both halves of an unused cheque can make one move. The rules exist to keep the halves apart.是不同的取捨，而且我們直說。比特幣的弱點是簽名裝置，之後是量子電腦。Bitcheque 的弱點是紙：持有一張未用支票兩半的人就能轉移一次。所有規則都是為了讓兩半分開。

Is there a token sale?有代幣發售嗎？

No. Coins come only from mining, with a reward that ramps from zero over the first 20,000 blocks so nobody gains from arriving first. Block 0 pays nobody.沒有。幣只來自挖礦，獎勵在前 20,000 個區塊從零緩步上升，先到者沒有好處。第 0 區塊不付給任何人。
